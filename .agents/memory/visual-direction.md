---
name: Island Dream visual direction
description: The intended visual identity for the browser home screen.
---

Island Dream’s home screen should feel like a bright tropical shoreline: dimensional daylight sky, warm sun haze, distant islands, and deep layered ocean movement behind the browser UI.

**Why:** The product identity is a browser that feels like entering a tropical place, so the background should read as a real seaside environment rather than abstract decorative waves or a decorative prop.

**How to apply:** Preserve strong readability for the search interface over the scene, keep the ocean and wave motion subtle enough not to compete with controls, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
